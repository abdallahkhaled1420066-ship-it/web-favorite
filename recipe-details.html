Koshary

Recipe ID: RF-001Serves 4Prep 45 min

A hearty Egyptian classic: rice, lentils and pasta layered and topped with tomato sauce and crispy fried onions.

Ingredients

IngredientQuantity
Rice1 cup
Brown lentils1 cup
Short pasta1 cup
Tomatoes4 large
Onions3 medium

Instructions

  1. Boil the lentils until tender, then drain.
  2. Cook the rice with the lentils and set aside.
  3. Boil the pasta in salted water and drain.
  4. Simmer the tomatoes with spices into a thick sauce.
  5. Fry the sliced onions until crisp.
  6. Layer rice, pasta, sauce and onions on a plate.
